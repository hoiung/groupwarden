# Member notice — template

A starting point for the notice a human admin posts (and pins, or puts in the group description) in every group before groupwarden moderates it. Replace everything in [brackets] and delete what does not apply. It is a template, not legal advice: check it against your own DPIA and legitimate-interests assessment.

---

**How spam is handled in [community name]**

To keep scams and spam out, [community name] uses an automated moderator, groupwarden, run by [controller: the person or organisation responsible] on [its own computer / a server it controls]. It is not run by WhatsApp or Meta.

**What it reads.** Every new message posted in the groups of this community: the text, captions, links, contact cards, polls and event links, and the display name of the sender. It moderates group messages only, and it cannot see messages posted before it joined a group.

**What it does.** When a post matches our spam rules (for example a crypto or investment pitch together with a link, a phone number or "message me"), it deletes the post, removes the sender and bans them from every group of [this community / this community and [other community]]. There are no warnings. A keyword on its own never gets anyone removed.

**The ban list is shared.** [If `bans.scope` is `all_communities`:] [community name] shares one moderator and one ban list with [other community]: someone banned in either community is removed from, and kept out of, both. [If `per_community`: delete this paragraph.]

**Who sees what.** Each action is reported to our admins in a private Telegram group, with the full deleted message and any attachment (photo, video or file) as proof. Only our admins are in that group.

**How long we keep it.**

- The copy of a deleted message and its attachment: [30 days] (the message text in the admins' Telegram group is removed after the same [30 days]; an attachment posted there is taken down after [24 hours]).
- The record of each action (who, which group, which rule, when): [12 months].
- The ban list: until an admin lifts the ban.
- Encrypted backups of these records: the last [14] nights.

**If you were removed by mistake, or your account was hacked.** You can contest a ban: contact [an admin / email address / form], say which group, and we will review it. A human admin decides, and can undo the ban in one step. If your account was taken over, secure it first (WhatsApp → Settings → Account → Two-step verification), then contact us.

**Your rights.** You can ask what we hold about you, ask us to correct or delete it, or object to this processing: contact [contact details]. If you are unhappy with our answer you can complain to the Information Commissioner's Office (ico.org.uk). More detail: [link to your privacy notice].
